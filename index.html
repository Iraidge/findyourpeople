          Beckett Careers 🚀

          Get career-ready, starting now.

          LBU's careers team can help you with CVs, cover letters, placements and more. Book a free appointment through MyHub.

          Book on MyHub ↗(opens in a new tab)

          What they can help with

          • 📄

            CVs

            Get your CV checked and learn how to make it stand out. They won't write it for you, but they'll help you nail it.

          • ✉️

            Cover letters

            Tailor your letter to each job and show why you're a great fit.

          • 🏢

            Placements

            Find and apply for work placements and get real experience before you graduate.

          • 💼

            Jobs & interviews

            Part-time work, graduate vacancies, application forms and interview practice.

          How to book an appointment

          1. Open MyHub and log in with your university username.
          2. Go to Appointments and choose the type of help you need.
          3. Pick a time from the calendar of available slots.
          4. Bring your CV, cover letter or questions. Even a rough draft helps!
          Log in to MyHub ↗(opens in a new tab) About Beckett Careers ↗(opens in a new tab) Placements info ↗(opens in a new tab)

          MyHub and Beckett Careers are official LBU services. This site doesn't collect any of your details. Booking steps are based on LBU's published guidance (Nov 2026) and may change.
